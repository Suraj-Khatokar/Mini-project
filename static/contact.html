Frequently Asked Questions

How do I verify that products are truly organic?
All our products undergo IoT sensor verification and blockchain tracking. You can scan the QR code on any product to see its complete journey from farm to you.
How can farmers join your platform?
Farmers can sign up through our website and undergo a verification process. We provide IoT sensors and training to ensure quality standards.
What areas do you currently serve?
We currently serve major cities across India and are expanding rapidly. Check our marketplace for delivery availability in your area.
How do you ensure product freshness?
Our IoT sensors monitor temperature and humidity throughout the supply chain. Products are delivered within 24-48 hours of harvest.
